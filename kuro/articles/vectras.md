***Can you Daily Drive Windows on Android? (w/ Vectras VM)*** is a YouTube video made by [AveryEclipse](AveryEclipse) for the [AstroNT](AstroNT) channel. It revolves around installing Windows 7 Ultimate x64 on Vectras VM, a QEMU frontend for Android, and then ""daily driving"" it by setting up, installing and trying various third-party programs, varying from simple to resource-demanding ones, and then benchmarking at the end. The phone used in this video is a Samsung Galaxy A26 5G (also known by its name of AveryPhone, Avery's main and most beefy phone at the time).

Avery initially tried Vectras VM in January 2026, however the app has been sitting unused on their phone ever since. The idea to finally make use of the app for a video came around late March 2026, in a busy IRL context for Avery at the time.

Initial recordings date back to 4 April 2026, when they set up the VM itself and installed Windows 7 on it, however recordings for the rest of the video on actually using it would only follow up between 20-27 April 2026. Editing would then take place between 2 and 8 May 2026, when the video was completed and uploaded to YouTube.

As of 15 September 2026, it is placed as the third most popular AstroNT video with 7,745 views, after ***[The Return of the Windows Store in Windows 8.1?! (8Store)](The_Return_of_the_Windows_Store_in_Windows_8.1?!_(8Store))*** and ***[Windows 10 Mobile on an Android Device but EVERYTHING Goes Wrong](Windows_10_Mobile_on_an_Android_Device_but_EVERYTHING_Goes_Wrong)***, the direct sequel to this video in close proximity.
