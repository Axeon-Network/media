***ChromeOS Flex on an "Uncertified" HP Laptop*** is a YouTube video made by [AveryEclipse](AveryEclipse) for the [AstroNT](AstroNT) channel, focused on installing and using ChromeOS Flex as a first-timer. It is the first "Wave 2" video to have been officially recorded, particularily between 6-11 March 2026, the first overall video to include real-life footage, and the first video to feature the [HPLappy](HPLappy), as well as the last AstroNT video to have been at least partially recorded on [TheHaswellLappy](TheHaswellLappy) to date.

That said, the video wouldn't be finished until 29 May 2026 when it was finally uploaded to YouTube, as the video itself was thought to be a huge editing project (which ended up not to be as editing was finished within days) but without high expectations that it would pay off as well as other videos that came in the meantime, such as [Windows on Android](Can_you_Daily_Drive_Windows_on_Android) and [its sequel](Windows_10_Mobile_on_an_Android_Device_but_EVERYTHING_Goes_Wrong) which have gotten over 14K views combined, compared to the roughly 1.5K views the ChromeOS Flex video has gotten as of 15 September 2026. In addition, another factor that added to the reasons the video was delayed for so long was Avery being rather busy IRL around mid-to-late March and April 2026.

The video itself is composed of multiple segments: the first segment involving creation of ChromeOS Flex installation media was recorded on TheHaswellLappy, then a short segment followed of a screen recording of HPLappy's screen from the Windows 11 IoT Enterprise LTSC 2024 installation it had. The actual installation process of ChromeOS Flex (as well as the final segment analyzing its partition setup from CachyOS's live session) is real-life footage taken with a smartphone using what Avery called an "improvised setup" using phone boxes and stands. The exploration segment was made using ChromeOS's built-in screen recorder functionality, initially using the Screencast app until figuring out how to record without it.
