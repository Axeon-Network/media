***Windows 10 Mobile on an Android Device but EVERYTHING Goes Wrong*** is an [AstroNT](AstroNT) video produced by [AveryEclipse](AveryEclipse). Released on 23 May 2026, it is the second most popular video on the channel with 7,820 views as of September 2026, behind ***[The Return of the Windows Store in Windows 8.1?! (8Store)](The_Return_of_the_Windows_Store_in_Windows_8.1?!_(8Store))***. At a length of almost 31 minutes, it is the longest AstroNT video.

The video is a direct sequel to the previous video, ***[Can you Daily Drive Windows on Android? (w/ Vectras VM)](Can_you_Daily_Drive_Windows_on_Android?)***, which used a QEMU frontend for Android known as Vectras VM to run Windows 7 Ultimate under a VM on a Samsung Galaxy A26 5G (also known by its name of AveryPhone, Avery's main and most beefy phone at the time).

# Making of
The initial idea of this video came up on 2 May 2026, just before editing started for the Windows on Android video, with the goal of getting Windows 10 Mobile run on Android inside QEMU while looking and feeling as native as possible on an Android phone.

Actual work started shortly after the Windows on Android video released on 8 May 2026 to a critical acclaim. Although the recordings for any footage that didn't make it into the final video, initial attempts first revolved around trying to get a real ARM32 Lumia FFU to boot under Termux `qemu-system-arm` using EFI (as Vectras did not support ARM32 emulation) to no avail, which is justified by those FFUs only being designed to work with real Lumia devices.

As such, on 12 May 2026, Avery switched to using an x86 Microsoft Emulator `flash.vhd` image of Windows 10 Mobile Fall Creators Update (build 15254), the final version to ever be released for Windows Phone. Recordings from this day onwards have been included in the final video itself. Termux with VNC display continued to be used. Avery initially attempted to boot the Windows 10 Mobile emulator image on UEFI, however kept hitting various Windows Boot Manager errors. UEFI was eventually dropped after it was discovered that the image was actually only designed to work with Legacy BIOS after a brief trial on VirtualBox.

However as time went on Termux QEMU with VNC display proved to be extremely slow, as oftentimes Windows 10 Mobile would stay stuck on the boot process for hours on end. Attempting various QEMU parameters wouldn't help either. As such, the experiment was moved to Vectras VM, where it would finally successfully boot to the home screen. From there on, some more performance tuning was done to make Windows 10 Mobile more usable and reliable.

Attempts were made on-and-off-camera to make Windows 10 Mobile on Vectras seem more native by trying various ways to enforce a different screen resolution/portrait mode and optimize for touch input instead of having to manually move the cursor, which has proved to be impossible thanks to limitations of both Windows 10 Mobile and QEMU, however it was possible to easily enable Windows 10 Mobile's touch navigation bar. This and early failed experiment attempts led to "...but EVERYTHING Goes Wrong" ultimately being included in the video title, inspired by *[Discord on Windows Vista but EVERYTHING Goes Wrong](Discord_on_Windows_Vista_but_EVERYTHING_Goes_Wrong)*.

In the meantime editing started on 17 May 2026, being done in parallel with recording the experiment itself which effectively wrapped on 23 May 2026 as the edited video was getting finalized, and was rendered and uploaded the same day, exactly 2 weeks after the original Windows on Android video.
