***Microsoft's Failed "User-Friendly" Shell for Windows (Microsoft Bob)***[^1] is a YouTube video made by [AveryEclipse](AveryEclipse) for the [AstroNT](AstroNT) channel as part of "Wave 2" of videos, released on 19 June 2026.

Intended to be an improved remake of *A Microsoft Bob Video*, uploaded to the [Lynxmic](Lynxmic) channel in October 2022, the video revolves around installing and exploring [Microsoft Bob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Bob), a "user-friendly" graphical shell for Windows made by Microsoft in 1995 and discontinued a year later after ending up being a commercial failure.

Additionally, later in the video a look is also being taken at Microsoft Great Greetings, the only add-on application package ever made for Microsoft Bob during its support lifetime.

As of 14 September 2026, it is the worst-performing AstroNT video since *[Installing Windows Longhorn via MS-DOS! (Post-Reset)](Installing_Windows_Longhorn_via_MS-DOS)* with just 117 views after 4 days, growing to a total of roughly over 300 views as of date.
