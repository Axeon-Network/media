***The Windows 95/NT 4 Shell on Windows NT 3.51! (NewShell)*** is the second [AstroNT](AstroNT) video ever released. The debut of [AveryEclipse](AveryEclipse) in the project, the video was released on 8 November 2025. It revolves around exploring versions 1 and 2 of NewShell, which is basically the shell from Windows 95/NT 4.0 brought to NT 3.51 (also known as the Shell Technology Preview). As of 17 September 2026, the video has exactly 800 views.

It was initially planned to become the first proper video of the AstroNT channel, however it was later swapped in the original video planning (colloquially and retroactively known as "Wave 1") with *[Running VMware Workstation 12 on Windows Vista!](Running_VMware_Workstation_12_on_Windows_Vista!)* as it was thought the NewShell video wouldn't be "good enough" for a first video.

Earliest recording tentatives for this video along with the original thumbnail date back to 19 September 2025, just two days after the AstroNT channel was created, all of this having been done by [KitSixtyFour](KitSixtyFour), at the time the sole operator of the channel. However the video had been re-recorded several times due to recording lag and other issues and by the time the perfect footage came along to put in editing software demotivation had already striked, the footage being just tens of minutes of primarily only exploration. As such, and because of the video being too basic, swapping it with VMware 12 on Vista was taken into consideration as early as 30 September 2025.

After AveryEclipse joined the AstroNT project on 26 October 2025, they took over the NewShell video idea, having been recorded once more, with the thumbnail being remade. It is the only video made by Avery for AstroNT with its original raw recording (i.e. the one that made it into the final video) being lost media, as the practice of keeping the raw recordings of each video on their side wouldn't start until the next video, *[Android, But It's on the x86 Architecture...](Android,_But_It's_on_the_x86_Architecture)*.
