***Android, But It's On The x86 Architecture...*** is the 3rd [AstroNT](AstroNT) video ever released, part of the initial wave of videos for the channel, and the second to be produced by [AveryEclipse](AveryEclipse).

First AstroNT video to ever be recorded on [TheHaswellLappy](TheHaswellLappy) (which would later be used for the [8Store video](The_Return_of_Windows_Store_in_Windows_8.1)), it showcases Android x86, specifically the version based on Android 5.1.1 Lollipop, running on VMware Workstation.

The video was originally supposed to be released on 17 November 2025 following [Kit](KitSixtyFour)'s *[Discord on Windows Vista but EVERYTHING Goes Wrong](Discord_on_Windows_Vista_but_EVERYTHING_Goes_Wrong)* video according to the Wave 1 planning, however a major recording incident in said video led to the two videos being swapped. As a result, the Android x86 video was released on 11 November 2025 in the place of Discord on Vista, which was ultimately released on 18 November 2025.

As of 14 September 2026, it is the shortest AstroNT video to date (being only 5 minutes and 36 seconds long), with a total view amount of 555.
